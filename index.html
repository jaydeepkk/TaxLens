Common tax traps
The ones most people don't see until it's too late
TaxLens flags common UK tax inefficiencies based on your PAYE income profile.

The £100k trap

Personal allowance tapers to zero between £100k–£125,140. Effective rate: 60%.

Pension relief gap

Higher-rate taxpayers can reclaim 40% on contributions. It doesn't happen automatically.

Bonus sacrifice

A cash bonus is fully taxed. Via pension sacrifice, you often keep significantly more.

Child benefit taper

Above £60k, child benefit is clawed back. Above £80k, it's gone entirely.

ISA deadline

£20k annual ISA allowance resets April 5th. Unused allowance is lost permanently.

Gift Aid uplift

Donations via Gift Aid reduce adjusted net income — potentially below the £100k threshold.

About

Why TaxLens exists

This isn't a corporate about page. It's an honest explanation of the problem we're trying to solve, what TaxLens is, and what it isn't.

01 — The problem

UK tax is complicated. Finding clear answers shouldn't be.

If you're a UK professional earning a reasonable salary, you probably know there are things you could be doing better with your tax position. But actually understanding how they apply to your specific income picture usually means an afternoon of this:

  • HMRC guidance pagesAccurate but dense. Written for compliance, not clarity.
  • Salary calculatorsShow take-home pay. Don't model pension, bonus or tax traps.
  • Reddit threads and forumsHelpful but fragmented. Buried in 3-year-old comments.
  • YouTube explainersGood for concepts. Rarely apply to your specific numbers.
  • Asking an accountantOften the right answer — but expensive for a first-pass question.

After all of that, most people still don't have a joined-up picture of their own income and tax position.

02 — Why TaxLens exists

One place. Plain English. Your numbers.

TaxLens was created to make UK tax concepts easier to understand — through educational simulations, plain-English explanations, and calculations based on publicly available HMRC rules.

A simple goal.

Our goal is to help UK earners better understand their income and tax position before making financial decisions — or before walking into a conversation with an accountant or adviser.

Not to replace professional advice. Not to manage anyone's money. Just to make the starting point less confusing and less expensive than it currently is.

If any of this sounds familiar — the googling, the fragmented answers, the vague sense that you're missing something — TaxLens was built for you.

Understanding UK tax traps

The personal allowance taper between £100,000 and £125,140 creates an effective marginal tax rate of 60% for UK PAYE earners in this band. TaxLens provides free educational simulations illustrating how pension contributions and salary sacrifice may affect adjusted net income based on publicly available HMRC rules for 2025/26.

Pension tax relief for higher rate taxpayers

UK higher rate taxpayers earning above £50,270 are entitled to 40% tax relief on pension contributions. TaxLens illustrates how pension contributions affect take-home pay and adjusted net income through educational simulations based on publicly available HMRC guidance.

Salary sacrifice and bonus planning

Salary sacrifice arrangements allow UK employees to exchange salary for pension contributions, potentially reducing income tax and National Insurance. TaxLens provides illustrative educational scenarios showing the difference between taking a bonus as cash versus via pension sacrifice, based on 2025/26 HMRC rules.
